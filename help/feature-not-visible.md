# Why can’t I see a Luckgram feature?

Most missing-feature cases come from availability rather than a damaged account.
First check whether the feature is [retired](../features/wallet.md). Updating or
buying a subscription does not restore a retired feature. Otherwise check these
causes in order.

1. **Platform:** confirm the feature page lists iOS, Android, or Desktop.
2. **Version:** update from the [official download page](https://luckgram.me/download).
3. **Plan or balance:** check whether the action requires Premium, Premium+, Gems,
   or another usage allowance.
4. **Region or store:** legal and distribution rules can hide a feature.
5. **Rollout:** beta and limited-rollout tools do not reach every account at once.
6. **Account state:** some tools require a completed Luckgram service sign-in,
   eligible Telegram account, or specific chat permissions.
7. **Network/provider:** AI, translation, and account-insight features can
   depend on services beyond Telegram messaging.

## Safe refresh steps

1. Confirm important drafts or local-only files are saved.
2. Close and reopen Luckgram.
3. Switch to the affected account again.
4. Check Luckgram settings for a disabled tab or feature toggle.
5. Update the app.

Do not delete the app, clear all app data, or sign out of every account before
reading [Before you reinstall Luckgram](app-problems.md#before-you-reinstall-luckgram).

If the feature is still missing, contact support with the platform, Luckgram
version, feature name, and a screenshot that does not expose phone numbers, login
codes, wallet secrets, or private messages.
