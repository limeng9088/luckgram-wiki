# Accounts and privacy

<!-- features:top -->
> The status of each feature on this page matches [Luckgram features](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/). [← Back to features](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/)
<!-- features:top:end -->

You turn each of these on or off in **Settings → Luckgram Features**. They change what Luckgram shows on this device. They do not replace your phone's screen lock or Telegram's two-step verification.

## More accounts {#accounts}

> **Available** · [See it on the features page](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/#accounts) <!-- status:accounts -->

Telegram allows 3 accounts on one device. Luckgram lets you sign in to **10** for free, and **as many as you like** with [Luckgram Pro](pro.md#pro).

1. Open the account switcher from the chat list or Settings.
2. Tap **Add Account** and sign in with a phone number.
3. Switch between accounts in the account switcher with one tap.

Each account keeps its own notifications. When you reach the free limit, the app tells you and offers a way to look at Luckgram Pro. If Pro ends, accounts you already signed in to stay signed in; you just can't add more until you are back under 10. For step-by-step help, see [Add and manage accounts](../getting-started/accounts.md).

## Double Bottom {#double-bottom}

> **Available** · [See it on the features page](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/#double-bottom) <!-- status:double-bottom -->

Set a second passcode. Unlocking Luckgram with it shows only the accounts you pick; your usual passcode shows everything.

1. Set an app passcode first in Telegram's **Settings → Privacy and Security → Passcode Lock**.
2. In **Settings → Luckgram Features**, turn on **Double space** (off by default) and set the second passcode.
3. Choose which accounts appear when you unlock with the second passcode.
4. Lock the app and unlock once with each passcode to check what shows up.

When you unlock with the second passcode, notifications from the hidden accounts don't appear, and nothing in Settings shows that a second space exists. Both passcodes are stored only as salted hashes. Turning Double Bottom off needs the main passcode.

## Hidden chats {#hidden-chats}

> **Available** · [See it on the features page](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/#hidden-chats) <!-- status:hidden-chats -->

Take a chat out of your list without deleting it or leaving the group.

- **iPhone and iPad:** swipe the chat left in the chat list and tap **Hide**.
- **Android:** press and hold the chat and tap **Hide** in the menu.
- To find them again, pull down at the top of the chat list until **Hidden chats** appears. Swipe left on a chat there to **Unhide** it.

Hidden chats don't appear in any folder, don't count toward the unread badge, and don't notify you of new messages by default (you can change that in Settings).

Hiding only affects the Luckgram chat list on this device. The messages stay in Telegram's cloud and show normally on your other devices and apps. A hidden chat is not a Telegram Secret Chat; its encryption doesn't change.

## Restricted channels {#unblock}

> **Available** · [See it on the features page](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/#unblock) <!-- status:unblock -->

Some channels and groups are hidden only in the App Store and Google Play versions of Telegram and show "This channel can't be displayed". In Luckgram:

1. In **Settings → Luckgram Features**, turn on **Store-restricted channels** (off by default).
2. Go back to the channel; it now opens normally.

The switch is free, permanent, and needs no website sign-in. It only lifts the store-version restriction: channels Telegram blocks for everyone, channels blocked by law where you live, and deleted or private content stay unavailable. What you choose to view is up to you; follow your local laws.

See also: [Why can't I see sensitive (18+) or restricted content?](../help/sensitive-content.md)

**Platforms:** iOS and Android.
