## Gallery video as round video {#round-video}

> **Available** · [See it on the features page](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/#round-video) <!-- status:round-video -->

1. Open attachments in a chat and pick **one** video from your gallery.
2. Press and hold the send button (or open the **…** menu) and choose **Send as round video**.
3. Drag and pinch inside the round frame to choose what shows. If the video is longer than 60 seconds, pick a part of up to 60 seconds on the timeline.
4. Tap send. Conversion shows its progress and can be cancelled.

It arrives as a round video message, just like one you film in the chat. The option doesn't appear when you pick several videos or a photo. Turn it off with **Gallery video as round video** in **Settings → Luckgram Features**.

## Call recording {#call-rec}

> **Available** · [See it on the features page](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/#call-rec) <!-- status:call-rec -->

1. Turn on **Call recording** in **Settings → Luckgram Features** (off by default). You'll be reminded that recording laws differ by country and region, and that you should tell the other person and get their consent first.
2. During a one-on-one voice or video call, tap **Record** on the call screen; the button turns red and a timer starts. Tap it again to stop.
3. When the call ends, the recording is sent as an audio file to **Saved Messages** (or a chat you choose in Settings).
4. With **AI transcript** on (on by default), the recording comes with a transcript and an AI summary, so you can search it and see what was agreed. The first time, you're asked to agree to sending the recording for AI processing.

Recordings are stored only in your own Telegram cloud (Saved Messages or the chat you chose). Group calls and live streams can't be recorded. Luckgram can't decide whether a recording is legal for you; treat recordings as sensitive.

## Storage {#cache}

> **Available** · [See it on the features page](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/#cache) <!-- status:cache -->

In Telegram's **Settings → Data and Storage → Storage Usage**:

- **Cache limit:** 1 GB, 2 GB, 5 GB, 10 GB, 20 GB, 50 GB or no limit. Above the limit, media you haven't opened for the longest time is cleared first.
- **Keep media:** 1 hour, 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 1 month or forever, set separately for private chats, groups and channels.
- **Per chat:** tap a chat in the list on the storage screen to give it its own keep time and see how much space it uses.
- **Clear now:** shows how much space will be freed before you confirm.

Media in Saved Messages and files that are still downloading are never cleared automatically. Clearing the cache removes only the copy on this device, not the messages in Telegram's cloud; media downloads again when you need it.

**Platforms:** iOS and Android.

## Can I call WhatsApp or FaceTime from Luckgram? {#can-i-call-whatsapp-or-facetime-from-luckgram}

No. Luckgram is a Telegram app: its voice and video calls are Telegram calls and reach only Telegram users.

- **To call a Telegram user:** open their profile and choose a voice or video call.
- **Contacts who aren't on Telegram:** you can invite them, but you can't call them from Luckgram.
- **"Doesn't accept calls":** their privacy settings don't allow you to call them.

## How do I post a story? {#how-do-i-post-a-story}

> **Available now.** This is a standard Telegram feature and works the same way in Luckgram.

Stories are a Telegram feature: Luckgram shows and posts them the way Telegram does and adds nothing of its own.

1. Tap **My Story** at the top of the chat list.
2. Take a photo or video, or pick one from your gallery. Add text, stickers and a caption if you like.
3. Choose who can see it: **Everyone**, **Contacts**, **Close Friends** or **Selected Contacts**; you can also exclude people.
4. Tap **Post Story**.

Stories last 24 hours by default. More duration options, stealth mode and other extras are **Telegram Premium** benefits (Telegram's own subscription); Luckgram Pro doesn't include them. Who can post and how many stories are set by Telegram for every app; Luckgram adds no limits.
