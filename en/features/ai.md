# Luckgram AI

<!-- features:top -->
> The status of each feature on this page matches [Luckgram features](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/). [← Back to features](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/)
<!-- features:top:end -->

Luckgram AI runs on Luckgram's AI service. The first time you use any AI feature, the app explains that the selected content will be sent for AI processing and sends it only after you agree. There is a daily limit; [Luckgram Pro](pro.md#pro) raises it and adds the premium model.

## AI assistant {#ai-chat}

> **Available** · [See it on the features page](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/#ai-chat) <!-- status:ai-chat -->

Tap **Luckgram AI**, pinned at the top of the chat list, to open the assistant. It looks like any other chat:

- Ask directly, or tap a shortcut above the input bar: **Summarize**, **Rewrite**, **Explain**, **Solve**.
- Answers appear word by word; tap **Stop** at any time.
- In any chat, press and hold a message and choose **Ask AI** to bring it (with links and file names) into the assistant.
- Conversations are stored on this device per account. Start new ones, browse and delete old ones.

## Chat summary {#ai-summary}

> **Available** · [See it on the features page](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/#ai-summary) <!-- status:ai-summary -->

When a chat has many unread messages, a bar at the top shows "N unread messages · Summarize"; **Summarize chat** is also in every chat's **…** menu.

The summary lists decisions, dates and open questions, each with a reference you can tap to jump to the original message. Ask follow-up questions at the bottom of the card, such as "what did the client agree to". Larger ranges and follow-up questions are Luckgram Pro benefits. The card is marked "AI-generated, may contain mistakes".

## AI replies {#ai-reply}

> **Available** · [See it on the features page](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/#ai-reply) <!-- status:ai-reply -->

In the input bar tap **+ → AI writing** and choose **Help me reply** at the top. From the recent conversation and whatever you've typed, the AI suggests 3 replies in the same language. Tap one to put it in the text field (you can undo); nothing is sent automatically. Tap **More** for a new set. It works with an empty text field too.

## AI writing {#ai-writing}

> **Available** · [See it on the features page](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/#ai-writing) <!-- status:ai-writing -->

Type a message in any chat, then tap **+ → AI writing** and choose an effect:

- **Polish:** clearer, smoother wording.
- **More formal** / **Friendlier:** change the tone.
- **Shorter:** keep the meaning, drop the extra words.
- **Fix grammar:** only spelling, grammar and punctuation.

The result replaces the text in the input bar, in the same language. Nothing is sent automatically; edit it further before sending if you like.

## AI agents {#ai-agents}

> **Available** · [See it on the features page](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/#ai-agents) <!-- status:ai-agents -->

Open **Agents** at the top of the AI assistant. Agents are assistants set up for one kind of task, such as a travel planner, copywriter, language teacher, researcher or coding helper. Pick one and chat with it like with anyone else, or **Add to chat list**.

You can also create your own: give it a name and write how it should answer (role, tone, what to avoid). Your own agents are stored on this device per account.

## Agent market {#agent-market}

> **Available** · [See it on the features page](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/#agent-market) <!-- status:agent-market -->

On the **Agents** screen, switch to the **Market** tab:

- browse by category (writing, study, work, life, coding, fun and more) or search at the top;
- open an agent to read what it does and see sample questions, then tap **Start chat**;
- agents you've used appear in the **Mine** tab.

Luckgram adds agents to the market on its servers, so new ones appear without an app update. Agents marked **Pro** need Luckgram Pro; free users can read about them and are offered an upgrade when they tap Start chat.

## AI images {#ai-image}

> **Available** · [See it on the features page](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/#ai-image) <!-- status:ai-image -->

In the AI assistant, tap **Draw** and describe the picture you want in one sentence. The image appears in the conversation. Then:

- **Send to…:** send it to any chat or channel;
- **Save:** save it to your photos;
- **Redraw:** make another version from the same description.

Images may not match the description exactly. Don't pass them off as real photos, and don't generate portraits of real people or copyrighted characters.

## AI posts {#ai-post}

> **Available** · [See it on the features page](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/#ai-post) <!-- status:ai-post -->

In a channel where you can post, type a topic in the input bar (for example "weekend sale, roses 20% off"), tap **+ → AI writing** and choose **Write a post**. You get an editable draft in the tone of the channel's recent posts.

Under the draft, choose **Shorter**, **More emoji**, **Translate** or **Rewrite**, then tap **Publish** (scheduled posting works too). Check prices, dates and addresses before publishing.

## Data sent for processing {#data-sent-for-processing}

Only what you hand to the AI is sent: your question, the messages you selected or the range you asked to summarize, and the recent conversation used for AI replies. Turning an AI feature on doesn't upload your chats. Luckgram may change the model or provider behind a feature.

AI output can be wrong, incomplete or made up. Check anything about money, health, law, safety or identity yourself. See [Privacy and data](../help/privacy-and-data.md).

**Platforms:** iOS and Android.
