# Discover

<!-- features:top -->
> The status of each feature on this page matches [Luckgram features](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/). [← Back to features](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/)
<!-- features:top:end -->

People Nearby and Square run on Luckgram's own servers. They are not Telegram features (Telegram retired its own People Nearby in 2024).

## Discover tab {#discover-tab}

> **Available** · [See it on the features page](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/#discover-tab) <!-- status:discover-tab -->

The tabs at the bottom are **Chats**, **Contacts**, **Discover** and **Profile**. **Discover** holds Square and People Nearby. If you turn either one off in **Settings → Luckgram Features**, its row disappears from Discover.

**Settings** moved into the **Profile** tab: open your profile and tap **Settings**; everything inside is the same as before. On iPhone the old **Calls** tab is gone, and recent calls are at the top of **Contacts**.

## People Nearby {#nearby}

> **Available** · [See it on the features page](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/#nearby) <!-- status:nearby -->

- **Hidden by default.** Others see you only after you turn on **Visible to people nearby** on the People Nearby screen. Before that, the app explains what is uploaded and then asks for location permission.
- **Only a distance range.** Others see a range such as "within 500 m" or "within 1 km", never your location. Your phone coarsens the location before uploading it.
- **Turns itself off after 24 hours.** You're reminded before it ends and can extend it. When you turn it off, sign out or unlink, you disappear from the list at once.
- **Link your account and set a username first.** When someone taps Message, your chat opens through your public username.
- **Report and block.** You can report or block anyone from their card. Once blocked, neither of you can see the other.
- Location is read once, when you open the screen, refresh or extend; never in the background.

## Square {#square}

> **Available** · [See it on the features page](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/#square) <!-- status:square -->

- **A public feed.** Square is a public feed every Luckgram user can see, with **Latest** and **Hot** tabs. Posts can have text, photos and short videos.
- **Posted from your current account.** Posts, likes and comments use the Telegram account you're signed in with; you can start a private chat from an author's profile.
- **Public as soon as you post.** New posts appear right away. Content that many people report is hidden until a person reviews it.
- **Photos and videos.** Media is compressed before upload, and metadata such as where it was taken is removed.
- **Report and block.** You can report posts, comments and authors. Once you block someone, neither of you sees the other's posts and comments, and you're hidden from each other in People Nearby.
- **Delete your data.** Delete your own posts and comments at any time, or everything you've shared in Square at once.

## Scan {#scan}

> **Available** · [See it on the features page](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/#scan) <!-- status:scan -->

Tap **⋮** at the top right of the chat list; **Scan** is at the top of the menu.

- A QR code (or `t.me` link) for a Telegram user, group or channel opens that profile, where you can add them, message them or join.
- A proxy link opens Telegram's usual Add Proxy prompt.
- A Telegram login QR code opens Telegram's usual "Log in on another device" confirmation.
- Any other web address is shown first and opens only after you confirm; plain text can be copied.

You can also pick an image from your gallery to read a QR code in it. **My QR code** at the bottom opens your own code for others to scan. The first time, the app asks for camera permission. Turn it off with **Scan and proxy settings** in **Settings → Luckgram Features**.

**Platforms:** iOS and Android.
