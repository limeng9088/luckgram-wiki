# What are People Nearby and Square?

These are planned Luckgram discovery features, run on Luckgram's own servers. They are not Telegram features. Telegram removed its own People Nearby in 2024.

## People Nearby {#nearby}

> **In development.** Luckgram does not have this feature yet. It is on the roadmap, and this section describes the planned behavior.

- **Off by default.** Others can see you only after you turn on **Visible to people nearby** on the People Nearby screen. Before that, Luckgram explains what it uploads and then asks for location access.
- **Distance bands only.** Others see a band such as "within 500 m" or "within 1 km", never your location. Your phone rounds your position to about 1 km before uploading it, and the server does not store exact coordinates.
- **Turns off after 24 hours.** You get a reminder before it expires and can extend it. When you turn it off, log out or unlink, you disappear from the list right away.
- **Linked account and username required.** To become visible, link your device to your Telegram account and set a public username. When someone taps Message, your chat opens through that username.
- **Report and block.** You can report or block from a profile card. Blocked people can't see each other.
- Luckgram gets your location once when you open the screen, refresh or extend, and never tracks it in the background.

## Square {#square}

> **In development.** Luckgram does not have this feature yet. It is on the roadmap, and this section describes the planned behavior.

- **Public posts.** Square is a feed of short text and photo posts that every Luckgram user can read, with Latest and Hot tabs.
- **Reading is open; posting needs a linked account.** To post, like, comment or report, link your device to your Telegram account first.
- **Reviewed before it's public.** New posts are reviewed first. If a post isn't approved, you see why and nobody else sees it.
- **Photos lose location data.** Photos are compressed and their EXIF data, including where they were taken, is removed before upload.
- **Report and block.** You can report posts, comments and authors. Blocking hides each other's posts and comments, and hides you from each other in People Nearby.
- **Delete your data.** You can delete your posts and comments at any time, or delete all of your Square data at once.
