# Luckgram Pro

<!-- features:top -->
> The status of each feature on this page matches [Luckgram features](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/). [← Back to features](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/)
<!-- features:top:end -->

Luckgram Pro is Luckgram's own membership and has nothing to do with Telegram Premium: Luckgram Pro doesn't give you Telegram Premium benefits, and Telegram Premium doesn't give you Luckgram Pro. Every Luckgram feature not marked Pro is free.

## What does Luckgram Pro include? {#pro}

> **Pro** · [See it on the features page](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/#pro) <!-- status:pro -->

Open the subscription page from **Settings → Luckgram Features → Luckgram Pro** to see the current benefits, when your membership ends and how many AI credits you have left. The benefits are:

- **AI agent market:** use the [agents](ai.md#agent-market) marked Pro.
- **More accounts and cloud backup:** sign in to as many accounts as you like (5 for free) and use [cloud backup](#cloud-backup) below.
- **More AI:** more AI chats per day, plus monthly AI credits for the **premium model** (1 credit per message). Choose Premium model in the model picker of the AI assistant or an agent; when the credits run out you're told, and the standard model is used again.
- **AI chat analysis:** [chat summaries](ai.md#ai-summary) cover more messages and accept follow-up questions.
- **Built-in translation:** more translations per day.
- **Pro community:** join the private group for Luckgram Pro members ([below](#pro-community)).
- **No sponsored messages:** Telegram's sponsored messages no longer show in channels.

The subscription page shows the exact limits and credits, which may change. It lists only benefits that are already live.

**Paying:** you buy Luckgram Pro for one of the periods listed on the subscription page and pay with Telegram Stars on Telegram's payment screen. Before purchases open, the button says "Coming soon". Each purchase covers one period and doesn't renew automatically. When it ends you're back on the free version; accounts you're signed in to stay signed in, but you can't add more than the free limit.

When a free user taps a Pro feature, an "Upgrade to Pro" sheet explains why; **See Pro** opens the subscription page.

## Cloud backup {#cloud-backup}

> **Pro** · [See it on the features page](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/#cloud-backup) <!-- status:cloud-backup -->

Back up your accounts to the cloud, encrypted, and restore them on a new phone without signing in to each one again. Backups live only in the cloud; there's no file export or import.

**Back up:**

1. Open **Settings → Luckgram Features → Luckgram Pro** and find **Cloud backup**.
2. Select the accounts to back up.
3. Set a backup password (at least 8 characters, entered twice).
4. Tap **Back up to cloud**. The page shows when the last backup was made and how many accounts it holds.

**Restore (works between iPhone and Android too):**

1. On the new device, sign in to any one account first. You can also start from **Restore from cloud** on the sign-in screen.
2. Tap **Restore from cloud** and enter the backup password.
3. Select the accounts to restore and tap restore. Accounts already signed in are skipped.

**Keep it safe:**

- The backup holds your accounts' sign-in credentials (not messages, contacts or media). **It is the key to your accounts:** anyone with the backup and the password can sign in to them.
- The backup is encrypted with your password on your phone before upload. The cloud keeps only encrypted data that Luckgram can't decrypt. The password is used only on your device and is never uploaded.
- **If you forget the password, the backup can't be restored.** A wrong password shows "Incorrect password", and each further wrong try makes you wait a little longer.
- **Weekly automatic backup** is available only if you also choose to remember the backup password on this device (in the system Keychain / Keystore). It's off by default.
- Tap **Delete cloud backup** when you no longer need it, and the data is removed from the cloud.

## Pro community {#pro-community}

> **Pro** · [See it on the features page](https://luckgram.cc/en/features/#pro-community) <!-- status:pro-community -->

Pro members tap **Join the Pro community** in **Settings → Luckgram Features → Luckgram Pro**. A one-time invite link opens Luckgram's private Telegram group for Pro members; confirm to join. If you're already a member, the group opens directly.

The invite works only once, so passing it on doesn't work. When your membership ends, you're removed from the group automatically.

**Platforms:** iOS and Android.
